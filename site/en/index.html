Welcome to Shinhyo

Established in 2017, Shinhyo Daycare Center respects older adults as individuals. Our approach connects physical, cognitive, emotional and social activities to support daily life in familiar homes and communities.

Our spaces
Activity, exercise and rest spaces for a comfortable day.

OUR APPROACH

Five reasons families
choose Shinhyo.

Attentive care today, with everyday life tomorrow in mind.

01

Supporting independence

We connect daily activities with cognitive, emotional and physical engagement, aiming to support independence and ease the care responsibilities of families.

02

Experience since 2017

Our experience includes caring for older adults who need daily assistance, including people living with dementia or Parkinson’s disease. We pay attention to individual needs and changes.

03

Familiar people, continuing care

Long-serving staff and a stable working environment help us understand daily habits and individual preferences.

04

Meaningful activities

Cognitive activities, crafts and physical activities are part of our program, with ongoing development aimed at supporting daily function and quality of life.

05

Connections with the community

Outings and visits to local restaurants help older adults continue to enjoy social contact.

04 / QUESTIONS

Questions
from families.

Contact the center for advice
about your individual situation.

Who can use the center?

We welcome enquiries from older adults who need care at home and their families. Eligibility depends on long-term care assessment, health and available dates.

Can we enquire without a long-term care grade?

Yes, you can ask about the conditions that may apply without a grade and what application guidance is available. Please confirm the details by phone.

Is transport available?

Transport is described for Songpa-gu and nearby areas of Gangnam-gu, Gangdong-gu and Hanam. The actual routes and times depend on where you live and must be confirmed with the center.

How much does it cost?

The 2026 Grade 5 fee guide is an example based on a standard 15% personal contribution. Actual amounts vary by grade, hours, days, contribution rate and meals. Please ask for an individual explanation.

How do we arrange a visit?

Please call +82 2 486 4578 before visiting. The Korean site lists consultation hours of 10:00–14:30; confirm a suitable time and available languages when you call. Do not post identity numbers or health information on public channels.
