SHINHYO DAYCARE CENTER

Personalized daily care

Care that connects everyday activities with cognitive, emotional and physical support.

Supporting everyday life

The center respects older adults as individuals and introduces an approach that connects daily activities with cognitive, emotional and physical engagement. It has activity, exercise and rest spaces.

Discussing your family’s needs

Explain the assistance needed with movement, meals and rest, together with familiar daily habits. The specific services and support available depend on individual needs and service conditions and should be confirmed with the center.

Consultation and next steps

Prepare your preferred days, care needs and any transport requirements. Ask about eligibility, documents, fees and a visit before discussing a care plan.

Please confirm current schedules, frequency and individual support with the center.
