Physical activities

Group activities that help older adults move in ways suited to their condition.

Moving together

The center introduces physical activities adapted to each person’s condition. Existing activity photographs show older adults moving a ball while seated.

Activities and space

Ask about the exercise space and group activities introduced on the website. The specific exercises, use of equipment, participation requirements and frequency should be confirmed with the center.

Before taking part

During the consultation, explain any walking assistance needs, recent falls, pain or precautions for exercise. The center will need to confirm what participation is suitable for the individual.

Please confirm current schedules, frequency and individual support with the center.
